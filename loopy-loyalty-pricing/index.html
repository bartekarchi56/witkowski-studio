Before you start

Questions owners ask us

Do my customers need to download an app?

No. Customers scan a QR code or tap a link and the card saves straight into Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, the same place they keep boarding passes and tickets.

How do my staff add stamps?

Staff log in to the Stamper app on a phone or tablet and scan the customer's card. Each person gets their own login, which is what the staff login limit on each plan counts.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Start on the plan that fits today and move up when you open another location or want to run a second offer. Your cards and customers stay where they are.

What happens when my free trial ends?

Pick the plan that fits and your cards keep working without a gap. Customers never see the switch.

What's the difference between a location and a card design?

A location is a physical site where stamps are handed out. A card design is a separate loyalty offer, like "buy 9 coffees, get the 10th free" and a second card for pastries.
