FAQ

Questions before you start

Do my customers need to download an app?

No. They scan your QR code and the card opens in their phone's browser. They can add it to their home screen in one tap.

How do my staff give stamps?

They open the stamper on their phone, point the camera at the customer's card and tap "Add stamp". If the camera isn't handy, they can type the 6-letter code under the QR.

What happens when a card is full?

The card shows "Reward ready". Staff tap "Give reward", the customer gets their treat and the card starts again from zero.

Can I run more than one card?

Yes. For example a coffee card and a lunch card. The number of cards depends on your plan.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes, any time from your dashboard. Your customers keep their stamps.
